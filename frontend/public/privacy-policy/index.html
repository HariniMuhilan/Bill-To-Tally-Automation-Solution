Privacy Policy

Application: BillBooks (Purchase Voucher Automation) • Effective Date: March 2026

At BillBooks (operated by Ashan Tech Labs), we respect the privacy of wholesale and retail merchants. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your data when you use the BillBooks mobile application and web dashboard.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to automate purchase voucher entry and manage your subscription:

  • Account & Merchant Profile: Name, shop/business name, email address, and mobile phone number for authentication and provisioning.
  • Purchase Bill Images: Photos and documents (PNG, JPG, PDF) of supplier invoices that you capture or select using your device camera or gallery for OCR line item extraction.
  • Extracted Accounting Data: Structured line items, supplier GSTIN, invoice date, invoice number, HSN codes, tax amounts (CGST, SGST, IGST), and totals.
  • Device Session Identifier: A non-hardware UUID (Device Session ID) generated during login to enforce single-device active session policy per merchant account.

Zero Personal Data Selling: BillBooks never sells, rents, or monetizes merchant financial records, contact lists, or supplier pricing data to third-party advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To process camera photos using PaddleOCR and Google Gemini Multimodal APIs to extract bill line items.
  • To compile and synchronize draft Excel workbooks (.xlsx) and Tally Prime/Zoho Books voucher formats across your authorized device.
  • To enforce single-device account authorization and prevent unauthorized multi-device account hijacking.
  • To provide administrator subscription provisioning and invoice review workflows.

3. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Service Providers

We use industry-standard cloud providers adhering to strict security standards:

  • Supabase: Encrypted PostgreSQL database and authentication engine with Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring strict isolation between shops.
  • Google Gemini API: Structured text parsing governed by enterprise API terms (user data is not used for model training).
  • Render: Backend API hosting with TLS encryption in transit.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. In compliance with Google Play Store Data Safety requirements:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time via User Profile → Delete Account inside the mobile app.
  • Web Deletion Form: You can submit a public deletion request at /delete-account/ without needing to log in.

Upon account deletion, all authentication records, uploaded invoice images, draft Excel sheets, and device tokens are permanently wiped from our production database and storage buckets.

5. Security

All data transmitted between the mobile application, web dashboard, and backend APIs is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS. Passwords and tokens are securely hashed and managed through Supabase Auth.
